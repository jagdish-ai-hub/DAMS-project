# Domain options for a free Indian mutual fund comparison and calculator site

Method note: all checks were made from a sandbox with a web fetch tool and web search. I could not query registrars, WHOIS, or the IP India trademark register. A DNS failure ("getaddrinfo ENOTFOUND") here is weak evidence only. It can mean the name is unregistered, or registered with no website. Nothing below says a domain is available unless stated as evidence. Where I have no evidence, I say "could not verify".

## 1. Is mutualfundcompare.in in use, and is mfcompare.in a close competitor?

### Takeaway
mutualfundcompare.in and mutualfundcompare.com did not resolve from here, and a search for the exact name returned no page from either. That is unverified, not proof it is free. mfcompare.in is clearly live and in use: "MF Compare", run by Aswin V (FundsApp Wealth Technologies), with the same keywords as your favourite. Confusion risk with mfcompare.in is high, so the name should be treated as a direct competitor.

- mfcompare.in is live. Its title is "MF Compare | Un-Cluttered Indian Mutual Fund Analytics & Comparison". It describes itself as a research tool that analyses AMFI NAV records, compares ₹100 growth curves, and offers "14 financial calculators". It credits NAV data to MFAPI and states it is not investment advice. It is credited to Aswin V, linked to aswinv.com ([MF Compare, mfcompare.in](https://mfcompare.in)).
- aswinv.com names Aswin V as a FinTech Product Manager at FundsApp Wealth Technologies. It lists "MF Compare", linked as the live site at mfcompare.in, with the description "Empowering investors with side-by-side mutual fund analytics and portfolio overlap insights" ([aswinv.com](https://aswinv.com)).

### Inferences
- Your favourite name and mfcompare.in target the same phrase ("mutual fund compare") and the same product type. A user who remembers "MF Compare" could land on either. Google may also treat them as near-duplicate brands.
- The mfcompare.in page captured here showed loading states ("Fetching live NAV performance", "Loading live schemes from AMFI"). I could not confirm its data quality. That is not a reason to use the name, though.
- Whether mutualfundcompare.in is registered by someone else cannot be settled without WHOIS. Treat it as "unverified" until you check WHOIS yourself.

### Gaps
- Could not run WHOIS or a registrar lookup on mutualfundcompare.in or .com. That is the only way to know who holds it, or whether it is parked for sale.
- Could not search the IP India trademark register for "MF Compare" or "Mutual Fund Compare". A trademark search is needed before any launch.

## 3. Do exact-match keyword domains still help Google rankings in 2026?

### Takeaway
Google's own current guidance says keywords in a domain name have "hardly any effect" on ranking, beyond breadcrumbs. Secondary blogs agree that exact-match domains give at most a small advantage, and that the 2012 update cut the weight of low-quality exact-match sites. Google does not appear to have issued a 2025 or 2026 statement changing this.

### Cited Findings
- Google's SEO starter guide says the keywords in the domain name "alone have hardly any effect" on ranking, beyond appearing in breadcrumbs. It advises choosing a site name that is "best for your business" ([Google Search Central, SEO starter guide](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/seo-starter-guide)).
- The same guide says TLDs such as .com or .org do not matter to Search, except that a country-code TLD can matter for targeting a specific country, and even then the effect is usually small ([Google Search Central](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/seo-starter-guide)).
- Matt Cutts' 2012 exact-match domain change affected about 0.6% of English-US queries. It hit mainly thin, old affiliate sites ([Emulent, exact match domain update](https://emulent.com/resources/the-complete-google-seo-algorithm-update-history/googles-exact-match-domain-update)). This is a secondary source quoting the announcement, not the original post.
- A 2026 blog says exact-match domains do not guarantee rankings, and that keyword domains may still help click appeal ([Fruits.co, "Do Exact Match Domains Still Work for SEO in 2026?"](https://fruits.co/news/do-exact-match-domains-still-work-for-seo-in-2026)). This is an SEO agency blog, so treat it as opinion.

### Inferences
- A keyword domain will not rank a site on its own. Content, technical quality and links matter far more. A keyword domain may still help a click-through decision, which is the one benefit the secondary sources agree on.

### Gaps
- No primary Google source on exact-match domains in 2025 or 2026 was found. The secondary blogs are weak evidence.

## 4. Brand name or keyword domain, for AdSense trust and returning visitors?

### Takeaway
I found no credible evidence that a keyword domain hurts AdSense approval, or that a brand name helps it. AdSense review checks domain ownership and policy compliance, not the domain's words. Evidence on returning visitors was not found.

### Cited Findings
- For AdSense for Search, Google verifies that you own the domain or can change its content, then reviews the site against AdSense and publisher policies ([Google AdSense help](https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/9130038?hl=en)). The page does not mention domain names or keywords.
- A commentary guide lists original content, policy compliance, a privacy policy that covers cookies and third-party data, and About and Contact pages as trust signals. It is a third-party commentary, so treat it as secondary ([theguidex.com, AdSense approval](https://theguidex.com/resources/google-adsense-approval)).
- A 2026 publisher guide reports rising manual actions on high-authority domains, and recommends separating affiliate or sponsored content onto subdomains. It concerns search rankings, not AdSense approval ([Medium, site reputation abuse](https://medium.com/predict/site-reputation-abuse-google-recovery-how-publishers-and-affiliate-networks-can-rebuild-trust-in-cf1b5a82c428)).

### Inferences
- For AdSense, the content, policy pages and clear ownership matter more than the domain's name. A brand name and a keyword name should be treated as equal on AdSense, as far as the public evidence goes.
- For returning visitors, a short memorable brand is easier to type and recall. That is a reasonable inference, but I did not find a study that measures it.

### Gaps
- No study found measuring returning-visitor rates by domain type. This needs a direct check, for example by testing the names with users.
- Could not confirm current AdSense approval rules beyond the help page above.

## 5. Which extension, and what does the .IN registry require?

### Takeaway
.in is the natural choice for an India-focused site, and the registry permits foreign and non-resident registrants, though with extra checks. Indian registrants need a government-issued ID, and registrars verify it through e-KYC. The registry's own advisory page returned a 404 when I fetched it, so the rules below come from registrar and secondary summaries, and must be checked on registry.in before buying.

### Cited Findings
- A July 2025 NIXI advisory, as quoted in search results, says there have never been directions limiting .IN to Indian nationals. Foreign nationals and entities are eligible. Non-residents must state a business connection or purpose in India ([registry.in advisory, cited in search](https://registry.in/registry-advisory-2025_07_29)). My fetch of this page returned a 404, so I could not read it directly.
- A registrar summary, effective 30 Nov 2025, lists accepted IDs for Indian registrants as passport, voter ID, driving licence, Aadhaar, PAN, or other Government of India ID. Resellers must verify and submit a confirmation of e-KYC ([Realtime Register](https://realtimeregister.com/blog/new-registration-rules-for-in-domain-names)).
- The same summary says the registry can accept or reject foreign registrations at its discretion, and that a missing declaration puts the registration on hold ([Realtime Register](https://realtimeregister.com/blog/new-registration-rules-for-in-domain-names)).
- Enom's policy page says Aadhaar and PAN fields are currently optional but expected to become mandatory for Indian individuals ([Enom, .IN policies](https://support.enom.com/support/solutions/articles/201000126061--in-domain-policies)).
- Privacy and proxy services that mask registrant contact details are not allowed for .IN ([Realtime Register](https://realtimeregister.com/blog/new-registration-rules-for-in-domain-names)).
- DomainIndia's knowledge base describes who can register a .IN ([DomainIndia](https://www.domainindia.com/client/knowledgebase/793/Who-Can-Register-a-.IN-Domain.html)). I did not read it in full.

### Inferences
- A .in domain is a good fit for an India-focused finance site. Its own registrant rules mean the owner should be a real Indian person or company with a verifiable ID. Plan for KYC before you buy.
- .com is fine as a backup, but mfcompare.com already responds from another owner. Buying .in plus a matching .com later is only worth doing if the .com is free.
- .app is not a country-targeted extension. Its price and rules were not checked.

### Gaps
- Could not verify .IN registration or renewal prices. The registry page did not load the pricing content.
- Could not read the registry's own advisory (404). The rules above should be confirmed on registry.in directly.
- Could not verify .co.in or .app registration rules or prices.

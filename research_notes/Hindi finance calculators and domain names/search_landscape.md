The WebSearch tool is described as US-only and offers no location or personalisation control, so the locale actually used is unconfirmed. ## Q5. Is there evidence of demand for fund-vs-fund comparison pages, and who ranks for them?

### Takeaway
There is supply evidence, not demand evidence. For "parag parikh vs hdfc flexi cap", several platforms (MySIPonline, Sharpely, Kotak Neo) have fund-pair compare pages, and INDmoney has pair pages for other fund pairs. I found no search-volume or traffic data for these queries, so demand strength cannot be stated.

### Cited Findings
- "parag parikh vs hdfc flexi cap" (EN, open): 9 results, of which 3 are compare pages from platforms ([MySIPonline](https://mysiponline.com/mutual-fund/compare/parag-parikh-flexi-cap-fund-vs-hdfc-flexi-cap-fund), [Sharpely](https://sharpely.in/mutual-funds/compare/hdfc-flexi-cap-fund-vs-parag-parikh-flexi-cap-fund), [Kotak Neo](https://www.kotakneo.com/mutual-funds/compare/parag-parikh-flexi-cap-fund-vs-hdfc-flexi-cap-fund/)), 3 are editorial or analysis pieces ([MySIPonline blog](https://blog.mysiponline.com/parag-parikh-flexi-cap-fund-vs-hdfc-flexi-cap-fund), [DSIJ](https://insights.dsij.in/dsijarticledetail/parag-parikh-vs-hdfc-flexi-cap-indias-two-largest-flexi-cap-funds-head-to-head-id010-56733), [Angel One](https://www.angelone.in/news/mutual-funds/analysis-of-india-top-flexi-cap-mutual-funds)), and the rest are a news page and a fund page.

# Override audit

The **Override Audit** screen is the one place to see every amount that was changed from what was originally entered — anywhere in the organisation. Use it to keep an eye on corrections without opening each entry.

## What's listed

- **Receipt line / Expense line** — an accountant changed a line's amount during review. Shows the line, the old and new amounts, and their reason.
- **Claim — final** — a finance manager closed a claim at a figure different from what was received. Shows the job card, the received total, the locked final amount, and the reason.

## Filtering

- **From / To** — the date range. Defaults to the last 90 days.
- **Branch** — limit to one branch.
- **User** — limit to one person's overrides (owner only).

Every row shows **when**, **who**, **was → now**, and **why**, newest first. Nothing here can be edited — it's a record.

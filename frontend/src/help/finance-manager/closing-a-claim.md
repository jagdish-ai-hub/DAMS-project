# Closing a claim

Warranty, AMC and goodwill (CG) claims are the jobs where the money comes from the manufacturer rather than the customer. You close these out, once the settlement is final. **Closing a claim is permanent — it can't be undone or edited.**

## Before you can close

Every receive document on the job card has to be **approved** first. If one is still in review, the claim can't be closed yet.

## Close a claim

1. On **Approvals & Claims** (Receipts tab), open an entry under **Open warranty / AMC / CG claims** — these are approved claim receipts waiting to be finalised.
2. Press **Close claim**.
3. The box shows **received so far** — the total of the settlement lines.
4. Enter the **final amount**:
   - Same as received — no reason needed.
   - Different — for example, Eicher settled short and you're accepting it — a **reason is required**.
5. Press **Confirm & close**.

## What closing does

- Writes a **locked final amount** for the job card.
- Marks every open receive document on that job card **settled**, which freezes those receipts.
- Locks the job card: category and business status can't change, no new receipt can be opened against it, and its Pending Amount reads 0.
- If the final amount differed from what was received, the record shows **"Overridden · Final"** with your reason everywhere afterwards, and the change appears on the **Override Audit** screen.

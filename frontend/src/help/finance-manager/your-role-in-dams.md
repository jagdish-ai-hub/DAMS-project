# Your role in DAMS

DAMS records every rupee each branch takes in and pays out. As **finance manager** you have the last word. An entry isn't final — and doesn't count in the owner's numbers — until you approve it.

## What you do

- **Approve entries** — everything the accountant has verified comes to your **Approvals & Claims** screen for final sign-off. You **approve**, **query**, or **reject**.
- **Close claims** — warranty, AMC and goodwill (CG) claims are settled by you, with a locked final amount, once every receipt on the job card is approved.
- **Watch the Override Audit** — every amount anyone changed from what was first entered is listed in one place for you to review.

## Where you sit in the flow

1. The **cashier** submits an entry.
2. The **accountant** verifies it (and may have overridden a line — you'll see the original struck through, with their reason).
3. **You** approve it. That's the point it becomes final and shows in the owner's dashboard. For an over-limit expense, your approval is also what lets the accountant close it.
4. For a **claim**, you close it at the end with the final settled figure.

## Good to know

- You **can't approve your own changes.** If you created or last touched an entry, another approver handles it.
- **Approving accepts every override on the record** as final — check the reasons before you do.
- **Closing a claim is immutable.** Once closed, the job card's final amount, category and status are locked and no new receipt can be opened against it.
- You see **every branch** — there's no branch filter on your role.

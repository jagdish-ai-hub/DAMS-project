# Chasing claims

Aging buckets show old claims. This page records the **next step** on each one — what, who owns it, by when — so claims stop dying from neglect.

## Steps

1. Open **Chase**. Overdue actions sort first.
2. Press into a claim (or record a new step with the job card number): the action, its owner (you or the Owner), and a due date.
3. Press **Done** when the step is complete. History is kept — it matters at write-off time.

## Good to know

- Only you and the Owner can own or close actions. The chase belongs to the claim owner.
- Pair this with the claim pack: chase the step, then upload through the Eicher portal in the same sitting.

# Closing the day

At the end of your working day you count the cash in the drawer and close the day on the **Cash** page. This is how DAMS checks that the notes in the drawer match what it thinks should be there, and it draws a line under the day so nothing can be slipped in afterwards.

## Before you start

Count the drawer properly first — all of it. You'll type the counted figure into the close, and it becomes tomorrow's opening.

## Close

1. Open **Cash** and make sure the date is the day you're closing.
2. Press **Close Day**.
3. The box shows the **computed position** — what the system expects to be in the drawer.
4. Type in the **physically counted cash**.
5. DAMS works out the **variance** (counted − computed):
   - **Zero** — nothing more needed.
   - **Not zero** — a **variance remark** is required. Explain why: a miscount, an entry you still have to add, cash handed out and not yet recorded, and so on.
6. Press **Close Day** to confirm.

## What closing does

- Saves the opening, computed position, counted amount, variance and your remark.
- **Locks the date** for your branch: no new cash movements on that day, and no cash-mode receipt or expense can be backdated into it.
- Sets **tomorrow's opening** to today's counted amount.

## Good to know

- You can't close a **future** date.
- You can't close a day that a later close already covers.
- Everything else about a closed day — its receipts, expenses, non-cash lines — stays visible and unchanged. Closing only locks *cash* for that date.

# Your role in DAMS

DAMS (Dealership Accounts Management System) is where your branch records every rupee that comes in and goes out. As a **cashier** you are the person who enters it all. Nothing reaches the accounts until you type it in, so your entries are the starting point for everything else.

## What you do in a normal day

- **Record receipts** — money customers pay you, against a job or an invoice.
- **Record expenses** — money you pay out to vendors and payees.
- **Record cash in / cash out** — moving cash between your drawer and the bank.
- **Close the day** — count the drawer and lock the date once you're done.
- **Fix anything queried** — if a checker has a question, it comes back to you.

## Where your entry goes after you submit

1. **You** create the entry and press **Submit**. It gets a permanent number (like `OOR-AUG26-R-001`).
2. **The accountant** checks it. They can **verify** it, **query** it (ask you a question — it comes back to you), or **reject** it.
3. **The finance manager** gives final approval. Only then does the money count in the owner's dashboard.
4. For **warranty / AMC / goodwill claims**, the finance manager also **closes the claim** at the end with a final figure.

So an entry moves **Draft → Submitted → Verified → Approved**. While it's a draft or queried, you can still edit it. Once it's verified or approved, the lines are locked — you can only add a new payment, not change what's there.

## Two things worth remembering

- **Your branch only.** You work in one home branch. Every entry you make belongs to it, and you can't record against another branch's job cards.
- **Cash means the drawer.** Only payments you take or make *in cash* change your drawer position. Card, UPI and bank transfers don't.

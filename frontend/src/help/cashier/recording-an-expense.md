# Recording an expense

An expense records money you paid **out** — petty cash, fuel, a taxi, a local purchase, a vendor bill. Every expense is billed to a **receiver** (whoever the money went to), and it either belongs to a job card or counts as branch overhead.

## Before you start

Know who you paid, how much, how you paid it, and whether it relates to a specific job. Have the bill or receipt ready to attach.

## Step 1 — open a new expense

From your home screen press **New Expense**. From a customer's page, **＋ New Expense** fills their details and offers their job cards.

## Step 2 — fill the header

- **Receiver Name** *(required)* — the person or firm the money actually went to. Type a new one and DAMS keeps it for next time.
- **Job Card** — pick the job this expense is for, or leave it as **branch overhead** if it isn't tied to one job.
- **Expenses Category** — from your owner's list. This decides which **sub-categories** you can pick on the lines.
- **Status** — where this expense is in its own handling (Open, Awaiting Receipt, …).

## Step 3 — add the expense lines

One **Expense Line** per payment made. For each: the date, the **Sub-category**, the **Amount**, and the **Payment Mode**. Bank and transaction ID appear only when the mode needs them.

If an amount is **above the sub-category's limit**, you'll see a warning. It still saves — but the finance manager will have to approve it before it can be closed.

## Step 4 — save or submit

- **Save Draft** — editable, no number yet.
- **Submit** — sends it for checking and locks the **DAMS-Expenses-ID** (like `OOR-AUG26-E-001`), which is permanent.

You can keep adding lines to an expense right up until the accountant closes it.

## Transfer to Claim

If the expense is on a **warranty / AMC / goodwill** job card, you can set its status to **Transfer to Claim** (or use the button when you re-open it). That means the cost is recovered from the company instead of paid as cash. It only works on a claim job card.

## Good to know

- You can only record expenses for **your own branch**.
- Only cash-mode lines affect your drawer — card, UPI and bank payments don't.

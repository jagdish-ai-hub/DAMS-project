# Fixing a queried entry

When a checker has a question about something you submitted, they **query** it. The entry comes back to you marked **Queried**, with their question attached. You make the correction and resubmit — it keeps the same number and picks up where it left off.

A query isn't a rejection. It just means someone needs you to check or change one thing before it can move on.

## Steps

1. Open **My Entries** from your home screen.
2. Queried rows are highlighted in amber and have a **Fix & Resubmit** button.
3. Press it — the entry opens in edit mode.
4. Read the query, then correct the header fields or the lines as needed.
5. If you think the entry was already right, add a **Remark** explaining why before you resubmit.
6. Press **Resubmit**.

The entry goes back to **Submitted** and returns to the checker's queue. Its DAMS-Receive-ID or DAMS-Expenses-ID does **not** change.

## Good to know

- Only **Draft** and **Queried** entries can be edited.
- Once an entry is **Verified** or **Approved**, you can still **Add Payment** to a receipt, but you can't change lines that are already there.
- A query can come from the accountant or, later in the flow, from the finance manager — the fix is the same either way.

# Cash in and out

The **Cash** page tracks the physical notes in your branch drawer. Sometimes you move cash between the drawer and the bank — that's what "cash in" and "cash out" record. They aren't customer transactions; there's no customer, vehicle or job card on them.

- **Cash In from bank** — you withdrew cash from the bank and put it in the drawer.
- **Cash Out to bank** — you took surplus cash from the drawer and deposited it.

## When you'd do this

The drawer is running low on change, so you draw ₹20,000 from the bank — that's a **Cash In**. Or the drawer has built up ₹80,000 and you deposit ₹60,000 of it — that's a **Cash Out**.

## Record a movement

1. Open **Cash** from the top menu. Check the date shown is the day you mean.
2. Press **＋ Cash In** or **− Cash Out**.
3. Fill in:
   - **Amount** *(required)*.
   - **Bank**, **Transaction ID**, **Remark** — the slip or transfer reference, and a short note.
4. **Save Draft** keeps it editable with no number. **Submit** sends it for checking and locks the **DAMS-Cash-ID** (like `OOR-AUG26-C-001`).

## The drawer position

The page always shows today's live position, worked out as:

```
Opening + cash receipts + Cash In − cash expenses − Cash Out
```

"Cash receipts / expenses" are only the receipt and expense lines you took or paid **in cash** — UPI, bank and card never touch the drawer. **Opening** is yesterday's counted close (or, on a branch's first day, the figure your accountant set).

## Good to know

- Cash movements are for **your branch only**, and you can't record one on a day that's already closed.
- These never show up as collections or expenses on the owner's dashboard — they only shift money between the bank and the drawer.

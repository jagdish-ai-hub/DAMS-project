# Quoting a job

A quote is the number the customer agrees to **before** the work starts. When the final bill differs from the quote, DAMS shows the variance on the spot — so billing disputes happen never, not at the counter.

## Steps

1. Open the customer's history and find the job card.
2. Press **Quote** — the estimate screen opens for that job.
3. Add one line per item (e.g. "Clutch plate", "Labour") with amounts.
4. Press **Save quote**. Quoting again later replaces the old one, but the old quote is kept as history.
5. The finance manager approves the quote. Only then is it the agreed figure.

## Good to know

- A quote never changes what the customer owes — only the final invoice does that. It informs the bill, like a budget informs spending.
- Rejected quotes come back with a reason. Re-quote with the correction.

# Finding a customer

Everything a cashier does starts from the **Find a customer** box on the home screen.

## Search

1. Type at least **2 characters** into the box.
2. You can search by:
   - **Name** — "Sumati", "Kalaivani Logistics"
   - **Vehicle number** — "OD05CA4177" (spaces and dashes don't matter)
   - **Job card number** — the Eicher / DBM number, e.g. "4009941587"
   - **Invoice number** — e.g. "7731122600388"
3. Results show one row per customer, with a tag saying which field matched.
4. Click a row to open that customer's history.

You only see job cards for **your branch**. If your dealership has turned on
multi-branch access for cashiers, you see every branch.

## The history card

The history screen shows:

- The customer's **vehicles** and phone number.
- **Job Cards / Total Invoiced / Total Received / Outstanding** totals.
- The list of **job cards** — category, status, invoice number, and amounts.
- A **payment timeline** once receipts have been recorded.

## Recently looked up

The four most recent customers you opened stay on the home screen as shortcuts
until you sign out.

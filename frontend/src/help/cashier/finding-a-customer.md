# Finding a customer

Almost everything you do starts by pulling up the right customer. The **Find a customer** box on your home screen searches across names, vehicles, job cards and invoices all at once, so you can type whatever the customer gives you and let DAMS work out the rest.

## Search

1. Click into the box and type at least **2 characters**.
2. It matches on any of these — you don't pick which:
   - **Name** — "Sumati", "Kalaivani Logistics"
   - **Vehicle number** — "OD05CA4177" (spaces and dashes don't matter)
   - **Job card number** — the Eicher / DBM number, e.g. "4009941587"
   - **Invoice number** — e.g. "7731122600388"
3. Each result is one customer, with a small tag showing which field matched — handy when you searched by vehicle but see a company name.
4. Click a row to open that customer's full history.

You see job cards for **your branch**. If your dealership has turned on multi-branch access for cashiers, you'll see every branch instead.

## The history screen

Once you open a customer you get everything about them in one place:

- Their **vehicles** and phone number.
- Totals: **Job Cards · Total Invoiced · Total Received · Outstanding**.
- The list of **job cards** — category, status, invoice number, and how much is invoiced / received / still due on each.
- A **payment timeline** of every settlement line recorded against them.

From here you can start a **New Receipt** or **New Expense** with the customer already filled in, or press **Add Payment** on a job card that still has a balance.

## Recently looked up

The customers you opened most recently stay on your home screen as shortcuts until you sign out — so going back to someone you were just dealing with is one click.

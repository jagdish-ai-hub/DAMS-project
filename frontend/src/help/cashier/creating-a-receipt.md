# Creating a receipt

A receipt is how you record money a customer has paid you — cash, card, UPI, a bank transfer, anything. One receipt covers one job or one reason for payment. If a customer pays for two unrelated things, that's two receipts.

You'll make receipts many times a day. It's the core of the cashier job, so it's worth knowing well.

## Before you start

Have the paperwork in front of you: the customer's bill or job card, and proof of how they paid (the card slip, the UPI reference, the cheque). You'll type a few details from each.

## Step 1 — open a new receipt

From your home screen press **New Receipt**. If you've already looked the customer up, open their card and press **＋ New Receipt** there instead — it fills their name in for you.

## Step 2 — fill the header

This is the "who and what" of the payment.

- **Customer Name** *(required)* — start typing and pick them from the list. If they're new, just type the full name and carry on; DAMS creates them.
- **Vehicle #, Job Card / DBM, Invoice #** — fill whichever you have. All optional, but they make the receipt much easier to find later.
- **Tran. Category** and **Status** — pick from the lists your owner set up (e.g. *Cash Sale*, *AMC*, *Warranty*).
- **Invoice Amount** — the full bill. Leave it blank if this is just an advance with no bill yet.
- **Transaction Type** — leave as **B2C** for a normal customer. Choose **B2B** only for a registered business, and then a **GST #** is required.

## Step 3 — add the payment lines

One **Settlement Line** per payment. Most receipts have just one, but if someone pays half by card and half in cash, that's two lines.

For each line: the date, the **Settlement Mode** (Cash, Card, UPI…), and the **Receive Amount**. Extra boxes for bank name and transaction ID appear only when the mode needs them — fill those from the slip.

Keep an eye on **Pending** at the bottom: it's the invoice amount minus what you've entered. Zero means fully paid.

## Step 4 — save or submit

- **Save Draft** — keeps it open for you to finish later. It has no number yet and nobody else sees it.
- **Submit** — sends it to the accountant for checking and locks in the **DAMS-Receive-ID** (like `OOR-AUG26-R-001`). That number is permanent and can never be reused, so only submit when you're sure.

## Good to know

- You can only create receipts for **your own branch**.
- After you submit, the accountant may **query** it — that just means they have a question. It comes back to your **My Entries** highlighted; fix it and resubmit.
- Attach the bill and payment proof in the **Documents** panel below the header — it saves everyone a phone call later.

# Asking another branch for a part

If your branch does not have a part but another one does, ask for it here instead of phoning around.

## Ask

1. Open **Spare Parts → Requests** and click **+ New request**, or click **Ask a branch** on the part's row in Stock.
2. Search the part. Only parts that some branch holds are offered.
3. Choose **which branch to ask**. You see what each one holds.
4. Enter the **quantity**, and the **job card number** if the part is for a job.
5. Click **Send request**.

Asking does **not** change anyone's stock. It only puts the request in front of the other branch.

## The three steps

1. **The other branch marks it Sent.** Their stock goes down by what they send, and the part shows as **on the road**. They may send fewer than you asked for, never more.
2. **The part arrives.** You click **Mark as received** on the request. Your stock goes up.
3. Done. The History shows both lines with the request number, for example `SP-12`.

> **Example.** Rayagada (OOR) needs a fender (IA309072). Jeypore (OOJ) holds 1. OOR asks OOJ for 1 → OOJ clicks **Mark as sent** (OOJ goes from 1 to 0) → the part travels → OOR clicks **Mark as received** (OOR goes from 0 to 1).

## If they cannot help

The other branch can **Decline** with a reason, and you can **Cancel** a request that has not been sent yet (also with a reason). Nothing moved, so no stock changes.

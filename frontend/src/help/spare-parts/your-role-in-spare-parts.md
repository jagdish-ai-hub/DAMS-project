# Your role in Spare Parts

Spare Parts keeps one list of the local spare parts held at **every branch**, so before anyone buys a part they can see whether another branch already has it.

You are either a **Store Keeper** or an **Inventory Manager**. Both of you can:

- **search** the stock of **every** branch, and see what each branch holds;
- see the **History** of every change (who, when, how many);
- **ask** another branch for a part, and **send** or **receive** parts for **your own** branch.

What else you can do depends on what your Owner has allowed. The usual setting:

| | Store Keeper | Inventory Manager |
|---|---|---|
| Bill a part to a job card | Yes | Yes |
| Ask another branch / mark sent or received | Yes | Yes |
| Upload the Excel sheet, add a part | — | Yes |
| Correct a quantity | — | Yes |
| Export to Excel | — | Yes |

If a button you expect is missing, the Owner has not allowed it for your role. They can change it in **Masters → Spare Parts**.

You act only at **your own branch**. You can look at the others, but you cannot bill from them or change their stock.

## One thing to remember

Nobody types over a quantity. Every change is a line in the **History**, so a mistake is fixed by a new line, never by erasing the old one. That is what makes the numbers trustworthy.

# Closing an expense

An expense closes by itself at the last approval step — you don't press anything extra. (Receipts are different: they settle themselves when the balance hits zero.)

- **Normal expense** (within its limit, ordinary status): it **closes the moment you verify it**.
- **Needs the finance manager** (over its limit, or a status like **Requires Finance Approval**): you verify it, and it **closes when the finance manager approves it**. If the finance manager already pre-approved it, it closes as soon as you verify.
- **Transfer to Claim**: see below — the finance manager closes it.

Once closed, the expense is locked — its lines and attachments can't be changed by anyone.

## Old expenses still at Verified

An expense verified before this change may still sit at **Verified**. Open it and press **Close expense** in the action bar.

## Expenses marked Transfer to Claim

If the expense is marked **Transfer to Claim** (money the manufacturer will pay back), you **don't close it**. Verify it, and it goes to the **finance manager**, who closes the claim and records the final amount recovered. **Close expense** isn't offered on it. If the finance manager queries it, it comes back to your queue — fix it and resend it.

## Good to know

- Check the amounts and the status before you verify: once it closes, nothing can be added or changed.
- If something's wrong at this stage, **query** it back rather than closing it.

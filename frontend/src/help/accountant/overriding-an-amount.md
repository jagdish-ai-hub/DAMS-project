# Overriding an amount

Sometimes a line's amount is simply wrong and you already know the correct figure — a transposed digit, a rounding slip. Rather than send the whole entry back to the cashier, you can correct that one line in place while the entry is still **submitted**. That's an override.

## When to override, when to query

- **Override** — you are certain of the right number and it's a small, clear-cut fix.
- **Query** — anything else: you're not sure, the whole line looks wrong, supporting paperwork is missing, or the cashier needs to understand what changed.

## Override a line

1. Open the entry from the queue.
2. In the lines table, press **Override** on the line.
3. Enter the corrected amount and a **reason** *(required)*.
4. Press **Save**.

From then on, everywhere that entry is seen, the line shows the **original amount struck through**, the new amount, and your reason.

## What an override means

- It's **provisional.** The finance manager still approves the entry, and sees your change and reason when they do.
- For an **expense**, the over-limit check is recalculated from the new figure — an override can push a line over its category limit, or bring it back under.
- For a **receipt**, the job card's Pending Amount updates straight away.
- Every override is listed on the **Override Audit** screen for the owner and finance manager.

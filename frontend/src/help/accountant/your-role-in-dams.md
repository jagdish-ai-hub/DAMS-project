# Your role in DAMS

DAMS is where every branch records the money it takes in and pays out. As an **accountant** you are the first checker. Cashiers enter the receipts, expenses and cash movements; you make sure each one is right before it goes any further.

## What you do

- **Review the queue** — every entry a cashier submits in your branches lands on your home screen. You **verify**, **query**, or **reject** each one.
- **Override an amount** — if a line's figure is wrong and you're sure of the correct one, you can fix it in place instead of sending it back.
- **Close expenses** — an expense document doesn't finish on its own; you close it once it's settled and the bill is in hand.
- **Review cash movements** — cash in / cash out gets checked the same way, on the **Cash** tab of your queue.

## Where you sit in the flow

1. The **cashier** creates an entry and submits it.
2. **You** check it. Verify → it goes to the finance manager. Query → it goes back to the cashier with your question. Reject → it's closed.
3. The **finance manager** gives final approval, and (for claims) closes the claim at the end.
4. The **owner** sees the money on their dashboard — but only once it's **approved**, not when you verify it.

## Good to know

- You **can't review your own work.** If you created or last changed an entry, another accountant has to verify it.
- You see entries only for the **branches assigned to you**.
- **Verify vs. Query vs. Override:** verify when it's correct as-is; query when the cashier needs to check or change something; override only when the number is wrong *and* you know the right one. When in doubt, query.

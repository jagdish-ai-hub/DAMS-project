# Awaiting bills

Some expenses are recorded before their bill arrives. They park on the **Awaiting Receipt** status — this page herds them, oldest first, so month-end isn't a bill-chasing scramble.

## Steps

1. Open **Bills** from your nav.
2. For each row, get the bill from whoever spent the money.
3. Open the entry and attach the bill. Move its business status off Awaiting Receipt.
4. The row disappears from this list.

## Good to know

- Tally entries stay provisional until the bill is attached — chase early, close calmly.
- If a bill will genuinely never come, query the entry back with a note instead of letting it age here.

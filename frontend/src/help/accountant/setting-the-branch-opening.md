# Setting a branch’s opening cash

A branch can’t **close its day** until it has a **starting cash balance**. You set it once, on the **Branch opening** screen. If the cashier sees *“not set — ask your Accountant”* on the Cash page, this is the fix.

## Set it

1. Open **Branch opening** in the menu.
2. Find the branch. A branch that still needs it shows **Not set**.
3. Enter **the date the drawer started** and **the cash counted in it**. If the drawer started empty, enter **0**.
4. Press **Save opening**.

The cashier can close the day straight away.

## Things to know

- **Pick the right date.** Cash receipts and cash in/out *before* that date are not counted in the drawer. Use the first day the branch took cash through DAMS.
- **It can be done once.** After it is saved — or after the branch has closed a day — it can’t be changed here. From then on each day starts from the day before’s counted close.
- **It can’t be in the future.**
- You only see the branches you are assigned to. The Owner and the cashier can see the amount, but can’t change it.

## A branch shows “Set”

Its amount and date are shown and locked. Nothing to do.

# Reviewing cash movements

Cash In and Cash Out movements go through the same review as receipts and expenses. On your **Review Queue**, switch the top toggle to **Cash**.

## What a cash movement is

It's a transfer between a branch drawer and the bank — cash withdrawn to stock the drawer, or surplus cash deposited. There's no customer, vehicle or job card on it, just an amount, a direction, and a bank reference.

## Work a movement

1. Click a movement. The right pane shows its **direction** (in or out), amount, bank, transaction ID, remark, and history.
2. Choose:
   - **Verify** — it's correct. It goes to the finance manager for approval.
   - **Query** — send it back to the cashier with a question. They fix it on the Cash page and resubmit.
   - **Reject** — it shouldn't stand. Give a reason.

## Good to know

- There are **no line-level overrides** on a cash movement — it's a single amount. If the amount is wrong, query it.
- Review state has never changed whether the cash was physically in the drawer. A movement counts in the drawer position the same way before and after approval — verifying and approving are about the paper trail, not the notes.

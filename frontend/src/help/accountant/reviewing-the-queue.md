# Reviewing the queue

Your home screen is the **Review Queue**. It shows every entry a cashier has submitted in your branches that's now waiting on you. Working through it is the main part of your day — until you act on an entry, it goes nowhere.

## Work an entry

1. Use the toggle at the top of the list to switch between **Receipts**, **Expenses** and **Cash**.
2. Click an entry. The right pane shows its header, every line, and its full history so far.
3. Decide:
   - **Verify** — the entry is correct. It moves on to the finance manager for final approval.
   - **Query** — you need the cashier to check or change something. Pick a **reason template** (missing bill, wrong category, missing DBM ref, amount mismatch, missing UTR) or write your own; the entry goes back to them and shows highlighted in their **My Entries**. When they fix and resubmit, it returns to your queue.
   - **Reject** — the entry shouldn't stand at all (a duplicate, or made in error). Type the reason. A rejected entry is closed for good; the cashier starts a fresh one if they need to.

## Triaging a long queue

Above the list you'll find three tick-boxes: **Over-limit** (expense lines above their sub-category limit), **Has override** (you or another accountant changed an amount), and **No bill** (the risk check found no attachment). Use them to work the risky entries first; tick several boxes on clean entries and press **Verify selected** to clear them together.

## Good to know

- You **can't review an entry you created or last changed** — a different accountant has to do that one.
- Verifying is **not** the finish line. The money doesn't count in the owner's dashboard until the finance manager approves it.
- If a line's amount is simply wrong and you know the right figure, you can **override** it instead of querying — see *Overriding an amount*.

## The overview

With nothing selected, the right pane summarises the queue: how many entries are waiting, their total value, and a branch-by-branch breakdown. Click any row there to open it.

# Your role in DAMS

DAMS hosts many dealership groups on one system. As **super admin** you sit above all of them. You don't touch any group's day-to-day accounts — you create the groups and control whether they can sign in.

## What you do

- **Onboard an organization** — create a new dealership group and its first owner.
- **Hand over the invite** — copy the owner's invite link and send it to them; there's no email in this version.
- **Deactivate or delete** an organization when a group leaves the platform.

## Where you stop and the owner begins

Once the owner accepts their invite and signs in, everything inside that organization is theirs: branches, staff, the master lists, and every transaction. You can't see or change any of it. Each organization is completely separate — nothing is shared between them.

## Good to know

- **Deactivate** blocks every user in a group from signing in but keeps all their data. **Activate** puts it back.
- **Delete** is permanent and removes *everything* for that group — branches, users, masters, job cards, documents, audit history. You have to type the organization name to confirm.
- The invite link is **single-use and expires.** If it lapses before the owner uses it, delete the organization and create it again.

# Turning Spare Parts on

Spare Parts is switched **off** for every organization until you turn it on. While it is off, nothing about it appears anywhere and the server refuses its requests, so it cannot affect an organization that is not using it.

## Turn it on

1. Open **Organizations**.
2. Find the organization and click its **Spare Parts** switch.
3. Read the confirmation and click **Turn on**.

The Owner of that organization now sees a **Spare Parts** menu and can create **Inventory Manager** and **Store Keeper** users in Team & Branches.

## Suggested order

1. Turn it on for the **demo** organization first and let your team try it.
2. When you are happy, turn it on for the client's own organization.

## Turn it off

The same switch. Nothing is deleted: all stock and history are kept and come back when you turn it on again. People with the Inventory Manager or Store Keeper role simply see no Spare Parts menu while it is off.

An organization that has stock history cannot be deleted. Deactivate it instead.

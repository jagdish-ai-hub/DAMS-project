# Your role in DAMS

DAMS is the accounts system for your whole dealership group. As the **owner** you don't enter day-to-day transactions — your team does that. Your job here is to set the system up for how your business works, and to read the numbers it produces.

## What you do

- **Read the dashboard** — collections, expenses, net, cash in hand, outstanding money, and recent activity, for the whole group or one branch.
- **Compare branches** — every branch side by side for the period you pick.
- **Manage team & branches** — add branches, invite staff, set who does what and where.
- **Manage masters** — the dropdown lists every screen uses: categories, settlement modes, banks, expense sub-categories and their limits.

## How the money reaches your dashboard

1. A **cashier** records a receipt, expense or cash movement and submits it.
2. An **accountant** verifies it.
3. A **finance manager** approves it — and closes warranty / AMC / goodwill claims at the end.
4. Once **approved**, it counts in your dashboard. Verified-but-not-approved entries show only as "pending review".

## Good to know

- **Cash in / cash out never counts as collections or expenses.** It only moves money between the bank and a branch drawer, so it only affects "cash in hand".
- **Nothing is hard-coded.** Every list your staff picks from comes from **Masters** — tailor it to your dealership.
- **You never delete people or list rows** — you set them **Inactive**. Past records always stay intact. Your group must always keep at least one active owner.

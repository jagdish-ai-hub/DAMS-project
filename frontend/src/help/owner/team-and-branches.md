# Adding branches and team members

## Add a branch

1. Open **Team & Branches**.
2. Under **Branches**, click **+ Add Branch**.
3. Enter the branch **name** (e.g. Koraput) and a **code** — 2 to 5 letters or
   digits (e.g. OOK). The code must be unique in your organization.
4. Click **Add branch**.

To change a name or code, or to deactivate a branch, click **Edit** on its row.
Deactivating hides a branch from new entries but keeps its past records.

## Add a team member

1. Under **Users**, click **+ Add User**.
2. Enter their **full name** and **email**. The email is their login — an
   invite link is created for them.
3. Pick a **role**:
   - **Owner / Finance Manager** — see every branch. No branch selection.
   - **Accountant** — tick **one or more branches** they will verify.
   - **Cashier** — pick **one home branch**. Every document they create posts
     under this branch.
4. Click **Add user**, then **copy the invite link** and send it to them. They
   set their own password from that link.

## Change or remove a member

Click **Edit** on their row. You can change name, role, branch access, and
status. Changing a role updates branch access to match.

Set a member to **Inactive** to stop them logging in — this never deletes
their past entries. Your organization must always keep at least one active
Owner.

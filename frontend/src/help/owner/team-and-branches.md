# Team & branches

The **Team & Branches** screen is where you set up the shape of your dealership in DAMS — the branches you operate, and the people who work in them.

## Add a branch

1. Open **Team & Branches**.
2. Under **Branches**, click **＋ Add Branch**.
3. Enter the branch **name** (e.g. Koraput) and a **code** — 2 to 5 letters or digits (e.g. OOK). The code has to be unique in your organization, and it's what shows on document numbers.
4. Click **Add branch**.

To rename a branch, change its code, or deactivate it, click **Edit** on its row. Deactivating hides a branch from new entries but keeps all its past records.

## Add a team member

1. Under **Users**, click **＋ Add User**.
2. Enter their **full name** and **email**. The email is their login, and an invite link is created for them.
3. Pick a **role** — this decides what they see:
   - **Finance Manager** — every branch, final approval, closes claims. No branch selection.
   - **Accountant** — tick **one or more branches** they'll review.
   - **Cashier** — pick **one home branch**. Everything they enter posts under it.
4. Click **Add user**, then **copy the invite link** and send it to them by your usual channel. They set their own password from the link.

## Let someone work in more than one role

Some people cover more than one job — an Accountant who also covers the cash counter at one branch, say. Instead of a second login, give them **extra roles**:

1. Click **Edit** on their row and tick **Allow role switching**.
2. Tick each extra role they may take on. For **Accountant** and **Cashier**, also tick **which branches**; **Finance Manager** works across all branches.
3. Click **Save changes**.

They then see a **⇄ Switch role** button next to **Help**. They pick a branch, then a role, and a bar under the header shows which role they're working in, with one tap to switch back.

What to expect:

- **Entries always carry their own name.** If Ajay switches to Cashier and records a receipt, it says Ajay. History and Override Audit also note "as Cashier".
- **They can't approve their own work.** An entry Ajay made as Cashier still can't be verified or approved by Ajay as Accountant.
- **One branch at a time.** As Cashier at OOR they post to OOR only. To work at another branch, they switch again.
- **You're never locked out of anything.** As Owner you always have the Switch role button and can work as any role at any branch.
- **Removing a role takes effect straight away.** If you take away a role while they're using it, they're sent back to sign in on their next click.

## Change or remove a member

Click **Edit** on their row to change name, role, branch access or status. Changing someone's role resets their branch access to match the new role.

Set a member to **Inactive** to stop them signing in — this never deletes their past entries. Your organization must always keep at least one active **Owner**.

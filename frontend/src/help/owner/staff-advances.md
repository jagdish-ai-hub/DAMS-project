# Staff advances

Salary advances go out, recoveries come back in, and the outstanding per staff is derived — never typed, so it can't drift from its own ledger.

## Steps

1. Open **Staff**. Add each staff member once (re-adding a name reactivates instead of duplicating).
2. Record **Advance out** when money is given, **Recovery in** when it comes back from wages.
3. The header shows each person's outstanding at a glance.

## Good to know

- A recovery can never exceed the outstanding — the entry is refused, not adjusted.
- Entries are never edited or deleted. A wrong entry is fixed by an opposing entry, like cash.
- Deactivating a staff member keeps their history but blocks new entries.

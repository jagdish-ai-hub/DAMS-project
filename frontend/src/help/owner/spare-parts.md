# Spare Parts

*This applies once your Super Admin has turned Spare Parts on for your organization.*

Spare Parts keeps the local spare stock of **every branch** in one place, so a branch can see what the others hold before it buys. You see the whole picture; your team does the work.

## Your team for this module

Two roles, set up in **Team & Branches** (pick the role, then tick the branches):

- **Inventory Manager** — uploads the stock sheet, corrects quantities, exports.
- **Store Keeper** — bills parts to job cards and sends or receives parts for their branch.

One person can look after more than one branch. A Cashier can also be given Store Keeper as an **extra role**, using the same *Extra roles* box as for the other roles.

## Decide what each role may do

Open **Masters** and scroll to **Spare Parts: what each role may do**. Tick or untick, then **Save changes**. It is checked by the server on every action, so unticking really blocks it. Both roles can always search every branch.

## Your own view

**Spare Parts → Overview** shows, across all branches:

- **Stock value** and units, and the value branch by branch;
- what was **billed this month**;
- **branch requests**: waiting, on the road, received;
- the **latest changes**, with who made them.

You are **view-only** here. To do something yourself, use **Switch role** and act as Inventory Manager at one branch, like any other role.

## History is permanent

Every change to a quantity is a line in **History** with who and why. A quantity is never typed over, so a mistake is corrected by a new line and the old one stays. That is what lets you trust the numbers.

# Reading the dashboard

Your home screen is the **Dashboard** — the group's money at a glance. Two controls at the top set what you're looking at: **branch** (or All branches) and **period** (Today or Month to date).

## The four headline numbers

- **Collections** — money received on **approved** receipts in the period. Entries still in review don't count here; you see those as the "pending review" figure under Cash in hand.
- **Expenses** — spend on **approved** or closed expenses in the period.
- **Net** — Collections − Expenses.
- **Cash in hand** — the live drawer position across the selected branch(es), right now.

**Cash In / Cash Out never appear in Collections or Expenses.** They only move money between the bank and a drawer, so they only change Cash in hand.

## The panels below

- **Collections vs Expenses** — a 14-day trend line. This always shows 14 days, whatever the period filter says.
- **Collections by mode** — the split across Cash / UPI / Bank / Card.
- **Branch comparison** — each branch's collections, expenses, net, cash, and last cash close side by side.
- **Expenses by category** — where the spend went.
- **Outstanding** — money still owed you: part-paid jobs, B2B credit invoices, and claims that are approved but not yet settled by the manufacturer.
- **Recent activity** — the latest submissions, queries, approvals and closes across the group.

## Reading it well

If a number looks low, check the period and branch filters first, and remember it only counts **approved** money — a busy day still in review won't show yet.

# Floor and renewals

Two read-only views over work already in the system. **Floor** shows what's sitting unbilled and why (oldest first, with stuck reasons). **Renewals** shows whose service or AMC is due — overdue plus the next 45 days.

## Steps

1. Open **Floor**. Anything older than two weeks is red — ask why it's stuck.
2. Record stuck reasons on the job card's history ("waiting for Eicher approval") so the board explains itself.
3. Switch to **Renewals** and press **Remind** to nudge the customer on WhatsApp.
4. Set the next service due date on every billed job card — empty means "no reminder wanted", never a default.

## Good to know

- This is visibility, not workshop management: no scheduling, no inventory.
- A lapsed AMC is revenue walking to a competitor. The renewals list is a sales list.

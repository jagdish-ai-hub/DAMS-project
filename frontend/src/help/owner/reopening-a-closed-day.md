# Reopening a closed day

If a cashier closes the day by mistake — too early, or with a wrong count — you can undo it. Only the Owner can.

## Reopen

1. Open **Cash** from the top bar and pick the **branch**.
2. Choose the **date** that was closed. The page shows who closed it and the counted amount.
3. Press **Reopen day**, type a **reason**, and confirm.

The date unlocks straight away. The cashier can add the missed cash entries and close again with a fresh count.

## Good to know

- Only the branch's **latest** close can be reopened. Each day's opening is the previous day's counted amount, so if a later day is already closed, reopen that one first.
- The original close — counted amount, variance, remark and who closed it — is kept in the audit trail together with your reason. Nothing is lost.
- You can view any branch's cash page, but **cash in/out and closing the day** stay with the cashier.
- Nobody else can reopen a day: not the cashier, the accountant or the Finance Manager.

## Expenses page

**Expenses** in the top bar lists every submitted expense across all branches. Filter by branch, state, date or search by document number or receiver, and open one to see its lines and history. You can change an expense's business status there; verifying, approving and closing remain with the Accountant and Finance Manager.

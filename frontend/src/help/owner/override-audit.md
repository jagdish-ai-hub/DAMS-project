# Override audit

The **Override Audit** screen lists every amount that was changed from what was originally entered — across all your branches. Open it when the numbers need an explanation: it shows **when**, **who**, **was → now**, and **why**, newest first.

## What's listed

- **Receipt line / Expense line** — an accountant corrected a line's amount during review. Shows the document, the old and new amounts, and their reason.
- **Claim — final** — the finance manager closed a warranty / AMC / goodwill claim at a figure different from what was received. Shows the job card, the received total, the locked final amount, and the reason.

## Filtering

- **From / To** — the date range. Defaults to the last 90 days.
- **Branch** — limit to one branch to compare correction patterns across branches.
- **User** — limit to one person's overrides.

Nothing here can be edited — it's a record. If one branch or person shows up far more often than the rest, that's usually a training issue (wrong category, missing bill photo) rather than a people issue — worth one conversation before anything else.

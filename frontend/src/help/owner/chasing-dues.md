# Chasing dues

The dashboard shows balances. This page owns the **next step**: every credit due gets a due date and the customer's promise, and the defaulter view ranks who owes most.

## Steps

1. Open **Dues**. Log a promise at billing time: document number, due date, what the customer said.
2. Work the **Overdue only** filter daily — oldest first.
3. Press **Remind** to send the due reminder on WhatsApp (needs the customer's phone on file).
4. Press **Collected** when the money lands — the follow-up closes.
5. Open **Defaulters** weekly to see total exposure per customer.

## Good to know

- Re-promising reuses the same row, so the reminder history survives.
- Overdue is computed live from the due date — it can never go stale.

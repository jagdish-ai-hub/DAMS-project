# Hindi calculators hide dates and assumptions

Hindi finance calculators are broad but thin where it counts: none of the calculator pages visited shows a government rate with its date, a range of outcomes, or a clearly stated formula convention, and several get the conventions wrong. That leaves a plausible gap for a neutral, dated, Hindi-first set of government-scheme calculators, though the gap is inferred from six pages and has not been measured for demand. On the domain, the favourite name mutualfundcompare.in should not be chosen until a WHOIS and trademark check clears it, because mfcompare.in is a live product with nearly the same name. Keyword domains do not help rankings much (Google says so), so a short, distinct .in name that passes a registry and trademark check is the better choice. Search results are held by brokers and banks, and no independent Hindi fund-comparison tool appears in the top results tested. Before building, read fincalculator's individual scheme pages, decide the name, and test AdSense-only revenue on a few pages. Findings below cite three research notes in the research folder: hindi_calculator_audit.md, domain_options.md and search_landscape.md.

## Coverage is broad, but dates and formulas are missing

Coverage is not the problem. **5paisa** lists **39 calculators** with Hindi labels, but its grid has no RD, SCSS or KVP ([5paisa index](https://www.5paisa.com/hindi/calculators); hindi_calculator_audit.md). **fincalculator.in** lists roughly **82** tools, including RD, SCSS, KVP and POMIS, though the same page gives two other counts ("86" and "70+") ([fincalculator](https://fincalculator.in/hi/investment-calculators); hindi_calculator_audit.md). Its individual PPF, POMIS, SSY and RD pages were never opened, so their outputs and rates are unknown.

The pages visited differ mainly in how they handle assumptions:

| Page | Strength | Weakness |
|---|---|---|
| [5paisa PPF](https://www.5paisa.com/hindi/calculators/ppf-calculator) | States a formula and 7.1% rate | Example matches start-of-year, not the stated end-of-year formula; monthly limit wrong; no date on 7.1%; OTP and brokerage push |
| [5paisa Parag Parikh SIP](https://www.5paisa.com/hindi/calculators/parag-parikh-sip-calculator) | Year-by-year table is internally consistent; disclaimer says results are "an indicator" | Blank defaults show "₹0000"; "PFAS" misspelling; "best PPFAS fund" FAQ despite a no-scheme-suggestion disclaimer; undated trailing returns |
| [Kotak SIP](https://www.kotak.bank.in/hi/personal-banking/investments/mutual-funds/sip-calculator.html) | Caution that the monthly obligation should not exceed monthly net income | Three outputs conflict (invested ₹10,000 against ₹50,00,000); headline "20%*" has no footnote; growth table empty; labels in English; data dated "Till July 2024" |
| [Angel One article](https://www.angelone.in/knowledge-center/mutual-funds/how-sip-calculator-works-hindi) | Worked example reproduces exactly; dated 5 June 2026 | Article with a "START SIP" box and phone field; calculator not embedded |
| [ICICI Direct article](https://www.icicidirect.com/hindi/ilearn/mutual-fund/articles/how-can-you-make-the-best-use-of-sip-calculators) | Explains a reverse, target-to-monthly mode | Dated 13 November 2021; defines an expected return rate it never uses in the formula |

The arithmetic bears this out. The 5paisa PPF example (₹1.5 lakh a year, 15 years, 7.1%) gives **₹40,68,209**. My re-run reproduces that figure exactly as a start-of-year calculation, while the stated end-of-year formula gives about **₹37,98,515**. The audit described the match as "close to" start-of-year, which understates it. Kotak's example (₹2,500 a month for 12 months at 1% a month) gives **₹32,023** on its own formula, not the page's ₹31,916. Angel One's example (₹10,000 a month, 12% a year, 10 years) gives **₹23,23,391** on monthly compounding with start-of-month payments, which is the page's figure. Angel One therefore shows the standard that is achievable.

None of the Hindi calculators visited shows a range, a probability band or a historical scenario. Each returns one assumed-rate figure, one sample, or one worked example. Several also carry sign-up or sales elements. fincalculator's index is the exception: it says "no signup needed", shows no lead form, and calls itself an independent private utility site (hindi_calculator_audit.md).

On government rates, the notes give a Q3 FY 2026-27 (October to December 2026) set reported as unchanged: **PPF 7.1%, SSY 8.2%, NSC 7.7%, KVP 7.5%, SCSS 8.2%** ([Free Press Journal](https://www.freepressjournal.in/business/small-savings-interest-rates-unchanged-for-oct-dec-ppf-at-71-per-cent-nsc-at-77-pc); [Angel One news](https://www.angelone.in/news/personal-finance/finance-minister-announces-small-savings-interest-rates-for-october-december-2026-quarter); [Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/personal-finance/story/small-savings-scheme-interest-rates-unchanged-for-q3-fy27-check-ppf-scss-ssy-rates-558892-2026-09-30)). The DEA listing for the revision is dated 30 September 2026 ([DEA](https://dea.gov.in/budget-division/475)), but the rate table itself was not opened. The 5paisa 7.1% matches, yet the page carries no date. SSY 8.2% rests on one source. The notes found no current POMIS or RD rate. The brief gives a 7.4% figure for the Monthly Income Scheme, which does not appear in any note, so this report leaves it out.

The gap the notes support is therefore specific: dated government rates tied to the quarterly notice, stated formula conventions, and range output. No Hindi calculator visited shows a rate date next to a government rate. This is an inference from six pages, not a market survey.

## Keyword domains add little, so distinctness decides

Google's starter guide says keywords in a domain name "alone have hardly any effect" on ranking, beyond breadcrumbs, and advises a name that is "best for your business" ([Google Search Central](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/seo-starter-guide); domain_options.md). The notes found no 2025 or 2026 primary statement changing that. A domain's job is therefore recall, distinctness and avoiding confusion with competitors, not ranking.

The favourite, **mutualfundcompare.in**, failed DNS resolution from the sandbox, and a search for it returned no page ([sandbox check](https://mutualfundcompare.in); domain_options.md). That is weak evidence: the name could be unregistered, or registered with no website, and WHOIS was not run. Meanwhile **mfcompare.in** is live as "MF Compare", credited to Aswin V of FundsApp Wealth Technologies, with about **14 calculators** and a title of "Un-Cluttered Indian Mutual Fund Analytics & Comparison" ([MF Compare](https://mfcompare.in); [aswinv.com](https://aswinv.com)). Both names describe the same product in the same words. I would not build on mutualfundcompare.in unless WHOIS shows it free, a trademark search clears "Mutual Fund Compare", and you accept that users will land on the live rival.

The notes checked eleven names, summarised here:

| Domain | Status in notes | Note |
|---|---|---|
| mfcompare.in | In use ("MF Compare") | Do not choose |
| mfcompare.com | Responds with HTTP 503 | Treat as taken |
| mfscreener.in, mfcalculator.in, sipcalculator.in | In use | Exact-keyword names already held |
| mutualfundcompare.in, mutualfundcompare.com | Did not resolve | Unverified; see above |
| mfcompare.co.in, sipcompare.in | Did not resolve | Too close to mfcompare.in |
| fundtulna.in | Did not resolve | Hindi "fund" plus "comparison"; descriptive, so harder to own as a trademark (my judgment) |
| niveshtulna.in | Did not resolve | "Nivesh" means investment; the notes say to avoid such words unless registered as an adviser |
| fundkhoj.in, sahifund.in | Not in the notes | Named in the brief only; no check at all. "Sahi" reads as "the right fund", which sounds like a recommendation (my judgment) |

The notes say the table has "five more" unresolved names, but the table lists six; I have used the table. The exact-keyword names are taken, which suggests generic keyword domains in this niche are crowded.

For an India-focused site, **.in** is the natural extension. Per the notes, foreign and non-resident registrants are eligible with a declared business connection, Indian registrants must verify a government ID through e-KYC, and privacy services that mask contact details are not allowed ([Realtime Register](https://realtimeregister.com/blog/new-registration-rules-for-in-domain-names); [registry advisory, as quoted in search](https://registry.in/registry-advisory-2025_07_29)). The registry's advisory page returned 404 when fetched, so these rules must be confirmed on registry.in before any purchase. Enom says Aadhaar and PAN fields are expected to become mandatory for Indian individuals ([Enom](https://support.enom.com/support/solutions/articles/201000126061--in-domain-policies)).

On AdSense, the notes found no evidence that a keyword domain hurts approval or a brand name helps it. Google's help page checks domain ownership and policy compliance, and says nothing about domain words ([AdSense help](https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/9130038?hl=en)). The notes found no study of returning visitors by domain type. If mfscreener.in is already your live domain, renaming it costs redirects and any ranking history it has; the notes have no data on either, and they do not state who owns mfscreener.in.

## Brokers and banks own the head queries

In the eight open search queries, the top results were broker, bank and AMC pages, explainers, and for one English query academic papers (search_landscape.md; example: [Bajaj Finserv compare tool](https://bajajfinserv.in/investments/mutual-funds-compare)). Independent tools such as mfcompare.in, mfscreener.in, MF Analyser, MFScope, OverlapIQ and Deepmoneyminds did not appear in any open result, though a domain-restricted search showed each is indexed. The named sites that did appear in open results were **personalfn.com, indmoney.com, mysiponline.com, sharpely.in and kotakneo.com**, most of them inside broker platforms. The Hindi results were explainers from brokers and banks, bank and broker calculators, one news-publisher calculator ([Aaj Tak](https://www.aajtak.in/business/tools-and-calculators/sip-calculator)), and a few off-topic pages. No independent Hindi fund-comparison tool appeared among the top nine for "म्यूचुअल फंड तुलना" ([5paisa Hindi compare](https://www.5paisa.com/hindi/mutual-funds/compare)).

The wording splits intent. "तुलना" (comparison) returns explainers, while "कैलकुलेटर" (calculator) returns calculators. The fund-pair query "parag parikh vs hdfc flexi cap" is different: MySIPonline, Sharpely and Kotak Neo each have pair pages ([MySIPonline](https://mysiponline.com/mutual-fund/compare/parag-parikh-flexi-cap-fund-vs-hdfc-flexi-cap-fund); [Sharpely](https://sharpely.in/mutual-funds/compare/hdfc-flexi-cap-fund-vs-parag-parikh-flexi-cap-fund); [Kotak Neo](https://www.kotakneo.com/mutual-funds/compare/parag-parikh-flexi-cap-fund-vs-hdfc-flexi-cap-fund/)), and editorial pieces rank near the top. The notes give no search volume for any of these queries, so **demand is unknown**.

The pair pages also disagree with each other. An Angel One analysis gives year-to-date returns of **22.08%** for Parag Parikh and **15.95%** for HDFC, while a Tickertape snapshot gives one-year returns of **35.98%** for HDFC and **29.60%** for Parag Parikh. The dates differ and neither was checked against a primary source. Expense ratios reverse order between Sharpely and Kotak Neo for the same pair (search_landscape.md, Q5). For a neutral site, dated and sourced fund data is a plausible edge. That is an inference.

## Why the field looks this way, and what it implies

Four explanations fit the notes, and none is confirmed. First, broker, bank and AMC sites carry large domains and attach calculators to account-opening funnels, so they can hold generic queries; the notes show the funnels but measure no authority. Second, mfcompare.in may load its data in JavaScript: its fetched text shows placeholders such as "Fetching live NAV performance" and "Loading live schemes from AMFI", so its raw HTML may hold little comparison text. That needs a raw-HTML check. Third, the exact-match names are already taken, so an independent entrant starts with no naming edge. Fourth, the snapshot comes from a US-only search tool with an unconfirmed locale and gives no Google position data, so it shows a pattern, not a ranking.

The implication is that a new site should not expect to win "mutual fund calculator" or "SIP calculator" soon. The better targets are the narrow Hindi queries the snapshot shows as thin: government-scheme calculators with dated rates, and fund-pair pages with sourced figures. Even there, the notes give no volume, so a demand check must come before scale. The AdSense plan also depends on traffic and earnings that are unproven. The notes did not examine AdSense's finance-category rules or what Indian finance pages earn per thousand views, and I have not checked either.

## Conclusion

The most useful finding is about where the gap sits. It is not in coverage, since 5paisa and fincalculator already list dozens of Hindi tools. It sits in the fine print of the calculator: a dated government rate, a stated convention, and a reproducible example. A builder can deliver all three on day one, and most visited pages deliver none. The domain question matters less than it seems. Keyword names are crowded and carry little ranking weight, so a short, distinct .in name that clears the registry and trademark checks is enough, and the favourite name carries a confusion risk that outweighs its appeal.

The plan should not be treated as confirmed until two things happen. Fincalculator's individual scheme pages need to be read, because they are the closest rival and were the one unread set. And the demand for the narrow Hindi scheme queries needs measuring, because the notes show thin competition in the snapshot but no search volume at all.
